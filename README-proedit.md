## How to Open
Double-click `proedit-studio-ultimate.html` in File Explorer — opens in your browser. No install needed.

## Key Features

### Photo Loading
- **📂 Open Files** — open individual photos (JPG/PNG/WebP/HEIC)
- **📁 Open Folder** — open an ENTIRE folder of photos at once
- Drag & drop also works
- Filmstrip at the bottom shows all loaded photos — click any to edit it

### One-Click Apply to All
- **⚡ Apply to All** (header + right sidebar) — applies every adjustment and filter from the current photo to ALL photos instantly

### Top 5 World's Most Popular Filters (Left Sidebar)
1. 🥇 VSCO C1 — Natural Beauty
2. 🥈 Clarendon — Bright & Vibrant  
3. 🥉 Teal & Orange — Hollywood Cinematic
4. 4️⃣ Golden Hour — Warm Natural Glow
5. 5️⃣ Film Noir — Dramatic B&W

### Right Sidebar Tabs
- **ADJUST** — Brightness, Contrast, Exposure, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks, Temperature, Tint, Saturation, Vibrance, Sharpness, Denoise, Clarity, Vignette, Grain, Fade
- **HSL** — Fine-tune each color channel (Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Aqua/Blue/Purple/Magenta)
- **CURVES** — RGB and per-channel tone curves with click-to-add control points
- **FILTERS** — 60+ filters (Instagram, VSCO, Cinematic, Natural, B&W, Vintage, Aesthetic, Bold)
- **TEXT** — Add text with font, size, color, shadow/glow/outline effects; click image to place
- **AI** — 100% local AI enhance (histogram analysis), portrait beauty, skin smoothing, auto levels, auto color, dehaze, 2× upscale
- **VIDEO** — Cinematic color grades, film grain, vignette, letterbox, Ken Burns, frame capture
- **EXPORT** — Original/HD/4K/8K, JPEG/PNG/WebP, export single or all

### Edit Tools (Left Sidebar)
- Crop with aspect ratio lock (Free/1:1/4:3/16:9/3:2/9:16) + rule-of-thirds grid
- Rotate 90°/180°, Flip H/V
- Add vignette shortcut
- Natural Beauty Mode (skin softening + warmth)

### Video Editor
- Load MP4/MOV/AVI — timeline appears at bottom
- Play/pause/seek, capture frames as photos
- Apply cinematic color grades in real time
- Letterbox/film grain/vignette/fade controls
